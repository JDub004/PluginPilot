# Competitive Analysis (Top 3)

Sources: see MARKET_RESEARCH.md.

## A. Nebenkosten-Check
| Alternative | Price | Strength | Weakness vs us |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain ChatGPT | 0 / Plus | Already used, conversational | No maintained rules or benchmarks. Misses deadline/CO2/HeizkostenV math. Not reliable |
| Mineko | 49–89 EUR, ~10 days | Legal-tech, letters, dispute support | Slow, expensive, separate site |
| Mieterverein | ~60–120 EUR/yr membership | Real legal advice | Membership friction, appointments |
| Lawyer | €€€ | Authoritative | Overkill for 280 EUR |
| Free calculators (CO2 split, benchmarks) | 0 | Single facts | Not a full workflow |

**Position:** instant, free first check inside ChatGPT with rule-backed findings; paid letter. Mineko and the Mieterverein become escalation paths (and possible partners), not enemies.

## C. E-Rechnung lesen & prüfen
| Alternative | Strength | Weakness |
|---|---|---|
