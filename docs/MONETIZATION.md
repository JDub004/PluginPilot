# Monetization

Constraint: digital goods and subscriptions cannot be sold inside ChatGPT (see MARKET_RESEARCH.md §2). All payment happens on our own site.

| Stage | Model | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | Free check in ChatGPT (acquisition + data on findings) | 0 |
| v1.1 | Objection letter + PDF report on our site, per use | 9–19 € (vs Mineko 49–89 €) |
| v2 | Landlord: create statement, per unit per year | 9–15 € per unit/yr |
| v3 | B2B white-label for Mietervereine, legal insurers, property managers | Contract |

Per-use fits a once-a-year job; subscription only makes sense on the landlord side.
