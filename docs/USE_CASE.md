# Use case

**Persona:** Lena, 31, rents 68 m² in Berlin. In September she receives the 2024 statement asking for a 412,40 € Nachzahlung due in 30 days. She doesn't know which items are allowed and doesn't want to pay 69 € for a check or join a Mieterverein.

**Today:** she Googles "Nebenkostenabrechnung prüfen", reads three blog posts, maybe asks ChatGPT, which gives generic advice, and pays anyway.

**With the plugin:** she photographs the statement and asks ChatGPT. The plugin finds Verwaltungskosten (144 €, never allocable), Kabel-TV after 01.07.2024 (54,30 € pro rata) and missing CO2 data (right to cut 3 % of heating = 23,77 €): **222,07 €**, with legal references and the deadline 2026-09-30. Golden test `realistic-berlin-2024`.

**Why ChatGPT is the right place:** the user already has the document and the question; the conversation explains the findings; the plugin adds what ChatGPT lacks: versioned legal rules, exact date and allocation math, and the CO2 stage table.
