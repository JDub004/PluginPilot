# Software-Stadt: product brief for the Systemhaus simulation (2026-10-05)

## What exists today (working, demo-able)
- Input: the client's software list (typed, pasted from Excel/CSV, or detected in an accounting/bank export: ~40 SaaS vendors).
  Optional: key persons with business contact data, sites (cities), external partners, contract end and notice period, importance 1-5.
- Output: an interactive isometric "city". Departments are districts, programs are buildings (type by category, height by users and importance).
  People walk around, there is a network map with real sites and partners, and you can jump between site towns.
- Deterministic "quests": duplicate tools, unused licences with € savings, critical data in Excel, no owner, shadow IT, data islands,
  unknown data flows, a key person without deputy, notice periods ending in ≤90 days.
- Every quest has a ready-to-copy draft (licence reduction request, cancellation letter, consolidation e-mail, AVV questions, checklists).
- "Werkzeuge" tab: deadline list, software inventory CSV, emergency contacts, management report (Markdown), all drafts. Offboarding checklist per person.
- Health score 0-100, savings/year, before/after comparison, share link (data only in the URL fragment, nothing stored), report with "erstellt von <Systemhaus>".
- Channels today: ChatGPT app (MCP), free; a web demo. **No** accounts, storage, reminders, M365/Google import, white label or multi-client yet.
- Sample company "Beispiel GmbH" (18 programs, 42 staff): health 49/100, 12,360 €/year savings found, 4,035 €/month software spend.

"In 30 minutes with your client you build their software city. Red buildings show savings and risks, which opens an advisory conversation
(licence consolidation, security, NIS2 documentation, emergency handbook). The report carries your logo."
